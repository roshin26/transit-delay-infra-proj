How freshness is measured

The data-freshness SLO says: the data should be no more than {{ fresh_seconds }} seconds old, for at least {{ slo_target }}% of the time.

After every successful fetch the data's age starts at zero and grows until the next one. Any time it spends above {{ fresh_seconds }} seconds counts against the target.

Not covered here yet: API latency and error rate. Those need Prometheus history and will appear in the Grafana dashboard.
